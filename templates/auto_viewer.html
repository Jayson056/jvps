Stream & Controls:

  • Isolated Cursor: By default in View-Only, your pointer moves locally as a blue virtual cursor without affecting the host's physical mouse.
  • Remote Control: Click Mouse: VIEW ONLY to toggle control. When enabled, clicks and drags interact with the host instantly with zero hover hijacking.
  • Sound: Live desktop audio and microphone are streamed in real time. Click Sound to mute/unmute.
  • Clipboard: Click Paste to Host (or press Ctrl+V while focused) to send text to host. Click Copy Host Clipboard to fetch host clipboard to your device.
